​Our club year begins in September, and we hold meetings at 7:30 pm on the third Thursday of each month until June.
Meetings at:
White Cliff Estate Rec. Center
2715 Dovely Park SE, Calgary

Directions to meetings:
From Deerfoot take exit 251 onto east bound Peigan Trail
Turn left on 26 St SE northbound
Turn right on 34 Ave SE (Intersection marked for Inglewood Golf to left)
Turn right at first cross street onto 26a St SE
Turn left onto Dovely Park SE
Destination on right

If you would like more information about G Gauge / G scale, visit your local hobby store or ask one of the members of the Rocky Mountain Garden Railroaders. They will be happy to tell you about the hobby and to show you examples of what they have done and how you can too. It really is easy to begin to enjoy the fun and excitement of modelling in “ G “.
