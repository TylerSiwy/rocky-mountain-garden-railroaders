Relax, Revive, then Repeat.


When used in a garden setting, the combination of soil, plants, rock and changing sunlight together with such options as smoke puffing engines and a sound system recreating a steam or diesel sound, bells, whistles and horns, combine to make the G scale experience one of incredible realism and personal satisfaction.

  A layout indoors is also a great source of relaxation for the whole family. Of course, a train running around the tree at Christmas time can bring back fond child hood memories for many of us.

 Garden railroading is a family hobby. Active in our club are whole families husbands-wives, parents-children, grandparents-grandchildren. Garden railroading is so large; more than one family member can contribute to a layout at any one time. Garden railroading is multi-disciplined; a successful garden-railroading project is one, which involves the use of a variety of differing skills and it is never, ever completely finished.

More Pictures

  RMGRR has grown through the years to include on average 35 families. Our activities go well beyond the Supertrain shows. We operate layouts when requested by hospitals, business interests or funds raising events. We have provided special event displays for Heritage Park at Christmas and Thomas Days. Aspen Crossing is the location of a Fathers Day weekend event. Our annual club layout tour is always a highlight as members’ visit and exchange ideas at each others layouts
