Our History

The Rocky Mountain Garden Railroaders [RMGR] www.rmgr.org had its beginnings in the late 1980’s. The start arose from a group of model railroad enthusiasts who got together one year to operate their own G scale trains at the Annual Calgary Model Railroad Show. The Super Trains Model Rail Road Show is held each April here in Calgary, Alberta, Canada.

   RMGR has grown through the years to include on average 35 families. Our activities go well beyond the train shows. We operate layouts when requested by hospitals, business interests or funds raising events. Our annual club layout tour is always a highlight as members’ visit and exchange ideas at each others layouts

Our Club

We believe that our hobby is a family activity suitable to all ages and a variety of interests. Therefore our annual dues are just $40 per family. This entitles you to enjoy our video library and access to expert modellers for assistance with your projects. Enjoy the fellowship as we form “work parties” to lay a members track and build mountains.
So plan on attending a meeting to get to know us.

Our Aims and Objectives

   Promote the hobby of Large Scale Model Railroading.
   Encourage and assist new members just getting into the hobby.
   Have fun, enjoy good fellowship while modelling and running our indoor and out of doors trains.
   Support each others endeavours with physical and technical assistance.
   Serve the public at Train Shows, Hospitals or retirement homes by operating a club Large Scale Train display.
